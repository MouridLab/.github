## Xam-xam bu yomb a jot te wóor

MouridLab ab mbooloom open source la buy def jumtukaay ak mbir yu ñeel **cosaanu xam-xamu murit yi**.

Sunu yéene mooy aar téere yi, leeral seen cosaan ak seeni dëkkandoo, ngir ku nekk man leen a jàng ci ay gongikuwaay yu leer te wóor.

## Li nuy tabax

| | Projet | Li muy amal | Tolluwaay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 📚 | **Mourid Library** | Dajale téere, sumb, nataal, mbindaat ak tekki ci benn téerekaay bu tërëlnu. | Mi ngi ci yoonu defar |
| 🔎 | **Mourid Search** | Seetlu mbind yi ci araab, wolof ak farañse, ci baat walla ci maana. | Mi ngi ci waajal |
| 🧠 | **Mourid RAG** | Joxe tontu yu man a delloo ci xaaj, téere ak sumb wi ñu jëfandikoo. | Jéemtu |
| 🔤 | **Mourid NLP** | Def jumtukaay yu ñeel OCR, umpiin mbind, méngale ak saytu làkki mbind yi. | Dina ñëw |

## Xarala gu liggéeyal gongikuwaay yi

Bépp mbir war nañu koo man a boole ak fu mu jóge :

```text
Téere → Sumb → Jukki → Mbindaat → Tekki → Done → Jumtukaay
```

Li nu fas yéene :

- **Toppandoo** — boole bépp mbir ak jukki ak sumb wi mu jóge.
- **Leer** — ràññee jukki cosaan wi ak njubbital, tekki walla coppite gu xarala.
- **Wóor** — wax bu leer li ñu wóorul ci moom, muy tur, bis walla beneen xibaar.
- **AI gu am teggin** — jëfandikoo xel mu defariku ngir gëstu, waaye du mukk gongikuwaay.
- **Ubbeeku** — bind te séddoo yoon yi, done yi ak jumtukaay yi, su sañ-sañ yi mayee ko.

## Bokk ci

Defarkat, gëstukat, taalibe, tekkikat, xamkat làkk, saytukat jukki ak defarkat nataal, ñépp man nañu ci bokk.

Mën nga jàppale ci kod, mbindaat, tekki, wut gongikuwaay, saytu ab xaaj, bind ay tegtal walla ci nataal.

1. Seetal repositoriy yi ak issues yi ubbeeku.
2. Seetal labels `good first issue` ak `help wanted`.
3. Xamal sa xalaat ci Discussions su laajee waxtaan lu jiitu.
4. Yónnee sa coppite ci pull request.

> Bépp ndimbal am na solo : benn gongikuwaay bu ñu gis walla benn njubbital man na yokk njariñu mbind yi.

## Joxe xalaatu projet

MouridLab man na dalal ay projet yu ñeel OCR araab, xaralay làkku wolof, dégtu, done, knowledge graphs, API walla ay jumtukaayu njàng.

Ci Discussions, leeral li ñu soxla, gongikuwaay yi am ak ñi koy jariñoo, ngir mbooloo mi man koo tabax ak yow.
